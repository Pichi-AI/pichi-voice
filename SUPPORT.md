# Pichi Voice support

Use [Pichi Voice issues](https://github.com/Pichi-AI/pichi-voice/issues/new/choose) for installation help, questions and bug reports. Search existing reports first. English and Russian are both welcome.

Include your platform, OS and app versions, selected model, the action you took and the expected result. Use a short synthetic example when possible.

Never post private recordings, transcripts, voice profiles, credentials, verification codes or unredacted diagnostic logs. Review screenshots for private meeting titles and account information.

Report suspected vulnerabilities through the [private security process](SECURITY.md). Community participation follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
