The model is published under Apache-2.0; inherited source-model attribution
and license are included in A1-TextPrivacy-NOTICE.txt and
A1-TextPrivacy-LICENSE.txt. A weights license does not erase training history.

The pinned disclosure records training on redmadrobot-rnd/pii_train,
Scanpatch controlled PII corpus, Nerus, iluvvatar/NEREL, and locally generated
templates and augmentations. The training mixture is not wholly synthetic.
The maintainer reports no explicit license in the pinned NEREL metadata and
an unresolved independent permission review. Nerus source-text rights were
not independently adjudicated. Permission for generated material and derived
weights was confirmed by the maintainer, but that confirmation does not
establish a NEREL license. The disclosure does not determine that use is
prohibited, and it does not establish that all training sources are cleared.

The maintainer also records masked-template overlap, development-set
similarities and repeated entity values. Fully decontaminated training and
formal privacy or memorization guarantees have not been established. The
source disclosure describes these findings and the distinct evaluation
sources in more detail. Pichi Voice does not redistribute the source corpora
or private evaluation text. Model weights are downloaded separately.
