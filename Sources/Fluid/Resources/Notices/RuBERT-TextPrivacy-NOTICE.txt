The model artifacts are downloaded separately. They are not included in this
repository. Their SHA-256 digests, lengths and revision are frozen in the
artifact descriptor. The private Pichi Transcribe PII fine-tune is not included
or redistributed by this feature.

This is a Russian news NER model for person entities. It is combined with
structured-identifier detection adapted from Pichi Transcribe. Organizations,
general locations, ordinary dates, drug names and ordinary long numbers are
not redacted by this privacy policy. Address masking requires a residence/home
address context. English person-entity recall is not established by this model.

Detection can miss sensitive data or mask non-sensitive words. The public model
is a small local candidate, not a claim of universal best quality. Synthetic
tests confirm its native execution and selected cases, not production recall
or performance on a particular iPad. Review exported text when needed.

The tiny-news model misses many names in lower-case, punctuation-free ASR text.
The existing generic Russian first-name lexicon, explicit person context and
patronymic/surname morphology therefore supplement model predictions. Inflected
first-name guesses require adjacent person evidence; a bare lower-case surname
suffix does not mask ordinary words. This layer is still fallible, especially
for names absent from the lexicon or without recognizable morphology/context.

Text redaction does not redact retained audio recordings. If audio storage is
enabled, the recording retains voices and spoken personal information.

Native inference uses the existing ONNX Runtime distribution, licensed in the
accompanying ONNXRuntime-LICENSE.txt notice.
