The package includes the author's exact window, filterbank and vocabulary.
All six files are pinned by SHA-256 in GigaAMModelCatalog. GPU is preferred;
CPU fallback uses the same published Core ML package.

The earlier v3_e2e_ctc.int8.onnx export remains registered for existing imports
and pinned jobs. Both public exports are Russian GigaAM v3 E2E CTC without a
private end-of-utterance (EOU) head.
The inherited settings identifier gigaam-v3-eou is retained for compatibility;
it does not identify an EOU head in this public artifact. Separately imported
private EOU exports have their own fingerprints and are not distributed here.

are included in GigaAM-LICENSE.txt. Model weights are downloaded separately
and are not included in this source repository. No endorsement by the
original authors or conversion author is asserted.
