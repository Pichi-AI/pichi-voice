The pinned upstream notice identifies this as a legacy artifact, separate
from the repository's Community-1 artifact set. Fluid Inference converted
the checkpoint to Core ML with fixed input shapes, fused the fbank frontend
into the embedding model, and palettized wespeaker_v2 weights to 8 bits.
The original conversion scripts were not preserved; the source notice
describes reconstructed lineage and output-parity evidence, not a fully
attested reproducible conversion. Pichi Voice consumes this pinned compiled
artifact through its existing voice-matching adapter and does not claim
to have trained or converted it.

Speaker embedding citation retained from the pinned model card:
Hongji Wang, Chengdong Liang, Shuai Wang, Zhengyang Chen, Binbin Zhang,
Xu Xiang, Yanlei Deng, and Yanmin Qian. "Wespeaker: A research and production
oriented speaker embedding learning toolkit." ICASSP 2023, pages 1-5, IEEE.
