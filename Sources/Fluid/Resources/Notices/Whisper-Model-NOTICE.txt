Whisper speech recognition models

Original model: OpenAI Whisper
Source and license: https://github.com/openai/whisper
License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI

The mobile provider offers Whisper Tiny. Its default Q8_0 GGUF export is
downloaded separately from handy-computer/whisper-tiny-gguf on Hugging Face:
https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/whisper-tiny-gguf
Legacy GGML imports are supported separately. The inference runtime has its
own TranscribeCpp and ggml notices.

The original model license is reproduced in Whisper-LICENSE.txt. The app does
not claim to have trained the original model or to be endorsed by OpenAI.
