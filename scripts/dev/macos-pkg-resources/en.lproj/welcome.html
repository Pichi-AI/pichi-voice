Your voice. Your workspace.

Welcome to Pichi Voice for Mac.

This installer places Pichi Voice in Applications, ready to open after installation.

Before you continue

  • Quit Pichi Voice if it is running.
  • macOS will ask for administrator authorization to install the app.
  • Your existing recordings, models and settings are preserved.

When the app opens, its guided first-run setup helps you choose a speech model and grant permissions. If you have already completed setup, you can pick up where you left off.

Select Continue to get started.
