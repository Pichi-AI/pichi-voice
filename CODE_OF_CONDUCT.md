# Code of conduct

Pichi Voice welcomes questions, bug reports and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. English and Russian are both welcome.

## Working together

- Be respectful and patient. Discuss the work and explain disagreements with concrete examples.
- Help others reproduce problems and understand proposed changes.
- Respect privacy. Share only recordings, text and other material you are authorized to publish.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual content and spam.
- Do not publish someone else's private information or continue unwanted contact.

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests and other community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@pichi.ai](mailto:hello@pichi.ai), the project's published support contact. Include links and only the information needed to understand the concern. Do not put private details into a public issue. Suspected software vulnerabilities belong in the [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

The project maintainer, [@sabbah13](https://github.com/sabbah13), reviews reports and may request changes, remove content, restrict participation or block accounts according to the severity and recurrence of the behavior. Reports should be handled with care for the privacy of the people involved.

## Правила общения

Пишите по-русски или по-английски. Обсуждайте проблему уважительно, без оскорблений, угроз, дискриминации и спама. Не публикуйте чужие личные данные. О нарушениях сообщайте приватно на [hello@pichi.ai](mailto:hello@pichi.ai).
